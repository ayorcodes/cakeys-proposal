02 / OverviewCakeys with love

What we're building

Orders currently come in through Instagram and WhatsApp, which is hard to keep on top of. This app puts ordering, payment and delivery updates in one place. Customers get a proper app with your name and colours, and you get one screen to run the kitchen from.

For your customers
  • Browse the menu and place orders any time
  • Pay securely in the app
  • Follow the order: accepted, preparing, on the way, delivered
  • Get a notification at each step
For you
  • New orders arrive instantly with a notification
  • Accept or decline, set the prep time, update the status
  • Pick a rider and the details go to them on WhatsApp
  • Add items, change prices or mark things sold out
The ordering and payment system behind the app is already built and tested. That means this project is mainly the app people see and use, connected to what already works behind the scenes, which keeps the timeline short.

07 / ScopeCakeys with love

What's included

One app for iPhone and Android
Built once, runs on both, branded to Cakeys With Love.
Customer ordering and checkout
Menu, product pages, cart and in-app payment.
Live status with notifications
Accepted, preparing, on the way, delivered.
Admin login on the same app
Run orders from your phone, no web dashboard.
Menu and price management
Add items, edit prices and prep times, mark sold out.
Rider assignment by WhatsApp
Pick a rider and the details are sent automatically.
App Store and Play Store submission
We handle getting it published on both.
30 days of support after launch
For fixes and small adjustments once it's live.
Left out on purpose
A few things people sometimes expect, and why they aren't here. Any of them can be added later if you decide you want them.
Live GPS map tracking. Status updates do the job without the extra cost.
A separate app for riders. WhatsApp already works for them.
A web dashboard. Everything runs from your phone.
Loyalty or points. Can be added in a later phase.

08 / TimelineCakeys with love

Timeline

Because the ordering and payment system is already built, this is mostly the app itself. Around four weeks from start to launch.

Week
1
Design and start the customer app
Agree the look, colours and screens, then begin building the customer side: menu, product pages and cart.
Week
2
Finish customer app, build admin
Checkout and order status on the customer side, then the admin side: orders, prep times, rider assignment and menu.
Week
3
Connect everything and test
Wire it to the ordering and payment system, then run real orders end to end and fix what comes up.
Week
4
Final checks and launch
Last round of fixes, then submit to the App Store and Play Store and hand it over with a quick walkthrough.
App review takes a few days on Apple and is usually faster on Google. That waiting time is part of week 4, so the launch date already allows for it.
